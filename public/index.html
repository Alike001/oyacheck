    02 / HOW IT WORKS

    Less guessing. More clarity.

    01

    Send the message

    Paste a suspicious link or ask a safety question. Speak or type in English or Pidgin. Voice stops after a short pause and ordinary questions auto-send; spoken links require review.

    02

    Look at evidence

    Exact known-domain matches and URL warning signals are kept separate. Sources are cited when available.

    03

    Ask what happens next

    Follow up in the same conversation. AI explains the report but can't change the verdict or invent a page inspection.
