CAREFUL WITH YOUR DATA

Keep your private details.

Your input goes to OyaCheck’s server for analysis. The checker doesn’t visit the submitted site or put your input in a public link, analytics or browser storage.

AI explanations are optional and use bounded findings, not the original URL’s path or query. Questions may be sent to Groq when configured. GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you check.

Open the checker →
Can I check any link?

You can examine one syntactically valid public HTTP(S) URL or domain at a time. Checks cover the address and text patterns, not the live website. Private addresses and unsupported schemes are rejected.

What does “no known threat found” mean?

The hostname is not in our small curated threat list and no configured warning pattern was found. New scams can pass these checks. It is never a safe verdict.

Is this only for Nigerian links?

No. General URL and message checks work independently of NCC. Nigerian advisories provide additional, narrow source evidence when a listed hostname matches.

Can it check a forwarded message without a link?

Yes. Choose forwarded message to examine wording patterns. OyaCheck cannot authenticate the sender, verify the story, or inspect attachments.

Do I need a wallet or pay to check?

No. The public checker needs neither. The existing buy integration only refreshes a fixed NCC advisory in a separately authorized local environment. It does not inspect your submitted website.
