Before you begin

A few good questions.

Open the checker →
Can I check links outside Nigeria?

Yes. General URL and wording checks work independently of NCC. Nigerian advisories add narrow published evidence when a listed hostname matches.

Will you open the link?

No. We parse the address and text; we don’t visit the submitted site, follow redirects, inspect forms or download attachments. Broader paid website inspection is not supported.

What if the message has no link?

Choose Forwarded message. We can flag wording patterns such as urgency and mentions of sensitive information. We cannot verify the sender or whether a story is true.

Do I need to pay or connect a wallet?

No. The public checker needs neither. Paid features are disabled in the public beta. The separate local buy integration only refreshes a fixed NCC advisory with explicit authorization.
